## 2. UPCOMING IPOs

*IPO = a company's first sale of shares to the public. Rumored = talked about, nothing filed. Confidentially filed = paperwork sent to regulators privately. S-1 filed = public paperwork. Priced = share price set, trading begins. Lockup = a period (often about 180 days) when insiders cannot sell.*

- Nothing new confirmed in these sectors. The latest IPO calendar I found (dated Nov 2) lists only a fintech (OPay) and a SPAC (a shell company that raises money to buy a business later). Neither fits our sectors.
- A very large AI-lab IPO is expected to crowd the calendar, but timing is unclear (late 2026 or later).
- [X-Energy (XE)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XE) — small nuclear reactor maker. Status: **priced** on Apr 24 at $23. By my own estimate (180 days) its lockup ended around Oct 21. Insiders may now sell shares, which can weigh on the price. No reactor is yet producing commercial power.
- Cerebras (AI chips) reportedly priced in May at $185 per share. I could not confirm its ticker, so I am not linking it.
- Fervo (geothermal power) and HawkEye 360 (defense radio-signal tracking) had filed earlier this year. I could not confirm whether they have priced, and I could not verify tickers.

## 3. SECTOR CATALYSTS

- **AI spending keeps rising.** One article says the big AI spenders put about $227 billion into data centers in the latest quarter, about 20% more than the quarter before. It puts the 2026 total near $803–828 billion. Capex means spending on buildings and equipment. Suppliers like Eaton, Vertiv and Entegris get that money. These figures are unverified; sources disagreed on Microsoft's number ($170B vs $190B).
- **Chip factory tax credit deadline.** From my earlier research: a 25% US tax credit for new chip factories requires construction to start by Dec 31, 2026. I did not re-confirm it today. It should push companies to commit to projects soon, which helps equipment and materials makers.
- **Rare earth supply.** A Pentagon contract is funding a US plant for rare earth metals (used in magnets for jets and missiles) ahead of 2027 rules that ban Chinese-origin materials in defense gear. I could not date the announcement.
- **Nuclear for space.** Canadian Strategic Missions Corp and Telesat signed a non-binding agreement in May 2026 on micro reactors for satellites. Early stage.
