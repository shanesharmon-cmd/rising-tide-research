<h3>1. NEW CANDIDATES</h3>
<p><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/LITE">Lumentum Holdings (LITE)</a></b> — Nasdaq, US — Semiconductors/optics — <i>established</i>.<br>
Makes the lasers and optical parts that move data between chips inside AI data centers. Search results say it and Coherent both report a shortage of laser chips that may last through 2027. When AI data centers expand, it sells more.</p>
<p><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/CR">Credo Technology (CR)</a></b> — Nasdaq, US — Semiconductors/connectivity — <i>established</i>.<br>
Makes active electrical cables (AECs, cables with a small chip inside that keep the signal strong) linking AI servers. One source says it holds about 88% of that niche and sales were up roughly 120%; I could not confirm those figures. More servers means more cables.</p>
<p><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/DFNS">T3 Defense (DFNS)</a></b> — Nasdaq, US/Israel — Defense — <i>moonshot</i>.<br>

It is a rebuilt company with an unproven track record, so treat it as high risk. I could not confirm whether it is profitable.</p>
<p><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MP">MP Materials (MP)</a></b> and <b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/USAR">USA Rare Earth (USAR)</a></b> were covered in the last digest, so nothing new as candidates. See catalysts below.</p>

<h3>2. UPCOMING IPOs</h3>
<p>Status terms: <i>rumored</i> (talk only, nothing filed); <i>confidentially filed</i> (paperwork sent to regulators privately); <i>S-1 filed</i> (the public paperwork a company files before going public); <i>priced</i> (share price set, trading starts). A <i>lockup date</i> is when company insiders are first allowed to sell their shares, which can push the price down.</p>
<p><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/NSCL">Nscale (NSCL)</a></b> — NYSE, UK-based — AI data-center builder — <b>S-1 filed</b> on Sept 18, 2026, not yet effective (not yet cleared to sell shares). It plans to list under NSCL. Pricing date and lockup not known.</p>
<p><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XLAB">Exascale (XLAB)</a></b> — Nasdaq (planned) — modular data centers — going public by merging with a SPAC (a pre-funded shell company that lists first, then buys a business). Expected to close in the second half of 2026; I did not confirm the date.</p>
<p><b>Recently priced (now trading):</b> <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/CBRS">Cerebras Systems (CBRS)</a>, an AI chipmaker, priced at $185 and raised about $5.6 billion. <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/FRVO">Fervo Energy (FRVO)</a>, geothermal power, raised about $1.9 billion. <h3>3. SECTOR CATALYSTS</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Big Tech spending:</b> One source (CreditSights) projects the top five cloud giants will spend about $602 billion on capex (money spent on buildings and equipment) in 2026, up 36% from 2025. That money flows to suppliers.</li>
<li><b>Power for data centers:</b> One report says US utilities plan about $1.4 trillion in spending through 2030, largely for AI. <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/DUK">Duke Energy (DUK)</a> reportedly plans $102.2 billion and <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SO">Southern Company (SO)</a> $81.2 billion. Transformers and switchgear (equipment that routes electricity) have long wait times, a bottleneck that favors makers like <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GEV">GE Vernova (GEV)</a>.</li>
<li><b>Nuclear (SMR, small modular reactors, compact factory-built nuclear plants):</b> <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SMR">NuScale Power (SMR)</a> and <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/OKLO">Oklo (OKLO)</a> remain the main pure-play names; GE Vernova is building a BWRX-300 reactor. Still early; no big new contract found today.</li>
<li><b>Rare earths:</b> A US–China truce reportedly continues, with talks on shortages of rare earths and other critical minerals. <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/USAR">USA Rare Earth (USAR)</a> won a Department of Energy award for a pilot rare-earth separation project. Beneficiaries also include <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MP">MP Materials (MP)</a> and <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/BHP">BHP Group (BHP)</a>.</li>
</ul>
