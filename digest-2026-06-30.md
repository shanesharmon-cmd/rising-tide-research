## SECTION 3 — SECTOR CATALYSTS

*Big forces — contracts, spending announcements, or policy shifts — that lift a whole sector.*

---

**Hyperscaler Capex Supercycle**
The five biggest cloud companies — Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Meta, and Apple — will collectively spend $602 billion on data center infrastructure in 2026, a 36% jump over 2025. Total global AI infrastructure capex (money spent on physical buildings and equipment) across all companies is estimated at $725 billion for the year. This is the single largest force lifting demand upstream across chips, cooling, power, rare earth materials, and nuclear energy.

---

**Nuclear Executive Orders — US Policy Green Light**
President Trump signed four executive orders in early 2026 to speed up reactor licensing, expand the domestic nuclear manufacturing base, and tie nuclear energy to national security. A direct policy tailwind for uranium suppliers like [Cameco (CCJ)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/CCJ) and component makers like [BWX Technologies (BWXT)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/BWXT). A go/no-go decision on the first commercial SMR plant in Romania (NuScale's RoPower project) is expected in the second half of 2026.

---

**Project Vault — $12B US Critical Minerals Stockpile**
The federal government launched a $12 billion program to buy and stockpile US-produced rare earth elements and critical minerals — acting as a guaranteed bulk buyer for domestic producers. Directly benefits [MP Materials (MP)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MP) by providing price support and demand certainty.

---

**China Rare Earth Export Restrictions**
China — which controls roughly 90% of global rare earth processing — restricted exports of key minerals in early 2026 as part of the US-China trade war. This created an immediate supply crisis for Western manufacturers. Western governments are urgently funding alternative supply chains, injecting policy money and investor attention into producers like [MP Materials (MP)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MP) and [Lynas Rare Earths (LYSCF)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/LYSCF).

---

**NATO 2% Spending Milestone — European Defense Surge**
For the first time in the alliance's history, all NATO members simultaneously met the 2% of GDP defense spending commitment. European defense budgets rose roughly 20% year-over-year in 2025–2026. Total global defense spending hit $2.63 trillion in 2025. This lifts mid-tier specialists like [Kratos Defense (KTOS)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/KTOS) and [Redwire (RDW)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/RDW) alongside the giant prime contractors.

---

**AI Power Crunch — Q1 2026 Tipping Point**
Q1 2026 was widely identified as the moment when AI infrastructure stopped being limited by available money and started being limited by available electricity. US data center electricity demand is expected to grow from 19 gigawatts in 2023 to 35 gigawatts by 2030 — equivalent to adding several new major countries' worth of electricity demand. An estimated $720 billion in grid investment is projected through 2030. This makes grid infrastructure, nuclear power, and data center cooling urgent necessities.
