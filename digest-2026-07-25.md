# Rising Tide — Morning Scan
**As of July 25, 2026** | Picks & shovels across AI, space, and technology infrastructure

This digest covers **upstream opportunities** — the suppliers, toolmakers, and infrastructure companies that giant AI, space, and tech players depend on. Think of it like a gold rush: instead of buying the mining company everyone is talking about, we look at who makes the pickaxes and shovels those miners need. When the big players grow, these companies grow with them.

## Section 1: New Candidates

*Each entry is tagged **established** (already makes real money) or **moonshot** (earlier-stage and riskier, may not yet be profitable).*

### Semiconductors & Chip Equipment

**[MKS Inc (MKSI)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MKSI)** — NASDAQ | Chip-making subsystems | **Established**

Doesn't sell finished chip-making machines — it sells the guts inside them: vacuum systems, lasers, optics, and power-delivery parts used by the big equipment makers like Applied Materials and Lam Research. When those companies sell more machines to build AI chips, MKS sells more of the parts hidden inside. Shares are up roughly 232% over the past year as chip-equipment demand has surged.

**[Onto Innovation (ONTO)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ONTO)** — NYSE | Chip inspection & metrology | **Established**

Makes the inspection and measurement tools (metrology means precisely measuring tiny dimensions) that catch defects in chips before they ship — both in the front-end wafer stage and in "advanced packaging," the process of stacking multiple chips together, which is essential for the newest AI chips. As chipmakers push into more complex packaging to keep up with AI demand, they need more of Onto's inspection gear.

### Power & Grid Infrastructure

**[GE Vernova (GEV)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GEV)** — NYSE | Grid equipment & gas turbines | **Established**

Makes the transformers, switchgear (equipment that safely routes and switches high-voltage electricity), and gas turbines needed to get power from the grid into data centers. Q1 2026 data-center equipment orders alone topped $2.4 billion — more than all of 2025 combined. Backlog (confirmed future orders) has swelled to $163 billion. The stock is up 76% in 2026 but trades at a rich valuation, so it's a name to watch rather than chase blindly.

**[Powell Industries (POWL)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/POWL)** — NASDAQ | Electrical switchgear | **Established**

A smaller, less-followed maker of switchgear and power-distribution equipment used in data centers, oil & gas, and utilities. Analysts note it has a strong balance sheet to keep investing as demand for grid and data-center power equipment keeps climbing — a potentially overlooked alternative to the mega-cap grid names.

### Robotics & Automation

**[Regal Rexnord (RRX)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/RRX)** — NYSE | Motors, actuators & gearing | **Established**

One of the few US suppliers building the motors, actuators, and tiny precision gears that go into humanoid-robot joints, alongside its long-standing industrial motor business. Automation orders were up 34% in the first quarter of 2026 as factories and robotics makers ramped up buying.

**[Vishay Precision Group (VPG)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/VPG)** — NYSE | Precision sensors | **Established**

Makes precision resistors, strain gauges, and load cells — the sensors that let robots and industrial equipment "feel" force, weight, and position. It's the top-performing stock this year among robotics-supply-chain names, up roughly 216% year-to-date, as demand for sensing hardware in automation and robotics has grown.

### Defense & Space Structures

**[Karman Holdings (KRMN)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/KRMN)** — NYSE | Missile, space & hypersonics components | **Established**

Builds structural and propulsion components for missiles, hypersonic vehicles, and space launch systems — a specialized manufacturing niche few companies can enter. It just agreed to buy a European missile-industry supplier, expanding beyond the US defense budget it currently depends on.

**[AST SpaceMobile (ASTS)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ASTS)** — NASDAQ | Direct-to-phone satellite network | **Moonshot**

Building satellites big enough to connect ordinary smartphones directly to space — no special equipment needed on the ground. The company reaffirmed 2026 revenue guidance of $150–200 million and is targeting about 45 satellites in orbit by year-end. Still early-stage and burning cash; a bet on demand for satellite phone coverage taking off.

### Rare Earths & Specialty Materials

**[Idaho Strategic Resources (IDR)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/IDR)** — NYSE American | Rare earth exploration | **Moonshot**

A small US mining explorer that just reported finding meaningful concentrations of heavy rare earths, including yttrium (a metal used in lasers, magnets, and camera lenses), at its Diamond Creek project in Idaho. Nothing is in production yet — this is early-stage exploration — but it's drawing interest from companies looking for rare-earth supply outside China.

## Section 2: Upcoming IPOs

*An **IPO** (Initial Public Offering) is when a private company sells shares to the public for the first time. **S-1 filing**: the formal paperwork a company files with the SEC (the US securities regulator) before going public. **Lockup date**: after an IPO, insiders are typically barred from selling their shares for ~180 days; when that expires, they can sell, sometimes pushing the stock price lower.*

### Recently Priced — Now Trading

**[Standard Nuclear (STDN)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/STDN)** — NYSE | Nuclear fuel manufacturer | Status: **Priced** (July 16, 2026)

Makes TRISO fuel and HALEU fuel — advanced, specially coated nuclear fuel pellets designed to withstand extreme heat without melting, used in small modular reactors. Priced its IPO at $15 per share, raising up to roughly $356 million at a valuation near $3.55 billion. This is a fuel supplier to the whole SMR industry, not a single reactor maker — a "picks and shovels" play on nuclear power. *Lockup date not yet confirmed; check before trading around it.*

## Section 3: Sector Catalysts

*Big-picture events, policy changes, or spending announcements that can lift an entire sector — not just one company.*

**🔹 ASML Raises 2026 Guidance for the Second Time This Year**

ASML, the Dutch maker of the machines that print circuits onto chips (the semiconductor industry's most critical single supplier), raised its full-year 2026 sales guidance to €43–45 billion, up from €36–40 billion, citing continued AI chip demand. When the industry's most important equipment maker raises guidance twice in one year, it signals strength for the whole chip-equipment supply chain, including [MKS Inc (MKSI)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MKSI) and [Onto Innovation (ONTO)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ONTO).

**🔹 Hyperscaler AI Capex Climbs Toward $725–750 Billion for 2026**

The largest tech companies (Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Meta) have all raised their 2026 spending plans again. Google alone now expects to spend $195–205 billion this year, up from $180–190 billion just last quarter. Every dollar of this spending eventually flows to chip suppliers, grid-equipment makers like [GE Vernova (GEV)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GEV) and [Powell Industries (POWL)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/POWL), and cooling companies covered in our prior scans.

**🔹 Rare Earth Prices Post Broadest Rally of the Year**

In July 2026, every tracked rare earth element rose in price, led by germanium (up ~28%) and dysprosium (up ~25%), for an average gain across the group of about 17%. This is described as the first broad-based rare earth price rally of the year, benefiting miners and processors including [Idaho Strategic Resources (IDR)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/IDR) and names covered in our prior scans.

**🔹 Rocket Lab's $8 Billion Bid for Iridium Reshapes the Space Sector**

Rocket Lab agreed to acquire satellite operator Iridium for about $8 billion in cash and stock, combining rocket launches with an existing global satellite communications network of over 2.5 million subscribers. The deal (expected to close in mid-2027) signals that space companies are consolidating to become full-service space operators, a trend that could lift valuations across satellite and launch suppliers like [AST SpaceMobile (ASTS)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ASTS).

**🔹 Tighter Rules on Chinese Critical Minerals in Defense Supply Chains**

A new executive order tightens the waivers that let defense contractors source critical minerals from China, raising compliance requirements not just for prime contractors but for smaller subcontractors too. This adds pressure — and opportunity — for companies with domestic or allied-country mineral and component supply chains, including [Karman Holdings (KRMN)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/KRMN) and rare-earth suppliers covered in this and prior scans.

**🔹 Fiscal 2027 Defense Budget Tops $1 Trillion for the First Time**

The proposed National Defense Authorization Act calls for $1.15 trillion in US military spending for fiscal 2027 — the first time the US defense budget has crossed the $1 trillion mark. This is a broad, multi-year tailwind for the entire defense supply chain, from prime contractors down to specialized component makers like [Karman Holdings (KRMN)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/KRMN).
