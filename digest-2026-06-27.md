# Rising Tide — Morning Scan
**As of June 27, 2026** | Picks & shovels across AI, space, and technology infrastructure

This digest covers **upstream opportunities** — the suppliers, toolmakers, and infrastructure companies that giant AI, space, and tech players depend on. Think of it like a gold rush: instead of buying the mining company everyone is talking about, we look at who makes the pickaxes and shovels those miners need. When the big players grow, these companies grow with them.

## Section 1: New Candidates

*Each entry is tagged **established** (already makes real money) or **moonshot** (earlier-stage and riskier, may not yet be profitable).*

### Semiconductors & Chip Materials

**[Entegris (ENTG)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ENTG)** — NASDAQ | Chip-making materials & filtration | **Established**

Makes the ultra-pure chemicals, filters, and containers (called FOUPs — wafer carriers that protect chips during manufacturing) that keep chipmaking clean. Every advanced AI chip requires its products. Shares rose ~14% in mid-June 2026 after strong semiconductor demand news and a new cross-licensing deal with JSR and Inpria — two Japanese materials companies — opening the door for collaboration on next-generation chip materials. When chipmakers spend more to build AI chips, Entegris wins almost automatically.

**[Coherent Corp (COHR)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/COHR)** — NYSE | Optical components for AI data centers | **Established**

Makes the fiber-optic transceivers (devices that send data through beams of light over fiber-optic cables) that connect thousands of chips inside AI data centers. As AI models get bigger and data centers grow denser, demand for faster transceivers keeps rising. Shares jumped ~13% after Coherent showcased 3.2-terabit (extremely high-speed data transmission) interconnects and high-voltage silicon-carbide power components at OFC 2026, a major optical-networking trade conference.

### Data-Center Cooling

**[Vertiv Holdings (VRT)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/VRT)** — NYSE | Power & cooling infrastructure | **Established**

Sells the power systems, cooling units, and monitoring software that keep data centers running. Q1 2026 revenue hit $2.65 billion — up 23% organically (meaning growth from the business itself, not from buying other companies). Its project backlog — confirmed future orders not yet delivered — more than doubled to $15 billion year-over-year. Revenue growth of 34% and earnings growth of 47% are projected for full-year 2026.

**[Modine Manufacturing (MOD)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MOD)** — NYSE | Thermal management systems | **Established**

A 100-year-old maker of heat exchangers (devices that move heat from one place to another) that has pivoted hard into AI data-center cooling. Data-center sales surged 73% in fiscal 2026 to over $1.1 billion. Recently signed a $4 billion long-term supply agreement with a major data-center operator — the largest deal in Modine's history. Expects data-center revenue to grow another 60–80% in fiscal 2027.

**[nVent Electric (NVT)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/NVT)** — NYSE | Data-center enclosures & cooling | **Established**

Makes the metal enclosures, cooling systems, and electrical connection hardware that protect and power equipment inside data centers. Infrastructure sales rose nearly 80% year-over-year in Q1 2026, with data centers as the primary driver. As AI racks get hotter (next-generation racks may draw 370 kilowatts of power — roughly equivalent to 300 hair dryers running at once), customers need nVent's liquid-cooling and power-distribution products.

### Rare Earths & Specialty Materials

**[MP Materials (MP)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MP)** — NYSE | Rare earth mining & magnets | **Established**

Operates Mountain Pass in California — the only active rare-earth mine and processing facility in the United States. Rare earths are a group of 17 metals used in the permanent magnets inside EV motors, wind turbines, robots, and guided missiles. China controls roughly 60% of global mining and over 90% of processing. In the second half of 2026, MP plans to begin selling finished rare-earth magnets for the first time, stepping up the value chain.

**[USA Rare Earth (USAR)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/USAR)** — NASDAQ | Rare earth processing & magnets | **Moonshot**

Focused on processing heavy rare earths (the more powerful and harder-to-find variety, critical for high-performance magnets) and making finished magnets domestically. In early 2026, secured $3.1 billion in total funding, including a non-binding letter of intent (a formal but not legally binding expression of interest to fund a project) for $1.6 billion from the US Department of Commerce. Pre-revenue but heavily backed by the federal government. High risk; high potential.

### Defense & Specialized Manufacturing

**[BWX Technologies (BWXT)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/BWXT)** — NYSE | Nuclear naval components (monopoly) | **Established**

The only US company certified to manufacture nuclear reactor components for Navy submarines — a national-security monopoly protected by extraordinary technical requirements and regulatory barriers. As the Pentagon expands the submarine fleet and interest in small modular reactors (SMRs — compact, factory-built nuclear power plants) grows, BWXT's specialized expertise becomes more valuable. Revenue comes from long-term government contracts.

**[Redwire (RDW)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/RDW)** — NYSE | Space hardware & defense components | **Established**

Builds solar arrays, cameras, robotic arms, and structural components used on satellites, the International Space Station, and defense platforms. Q4 2025 revenue grew 56% year-over-year. Full-year 2026 guidance is $450–500 million. Record backlog (confirmed future orders) of $411 million. Less covered by Wall Street than defense primes, which may leave it undervalued.

**[AeroVironment (AVAV)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AVAV)** — NASDAQ | Loitering munitions & drone systems | **Established**

Makes Switchblade loitering munitions — small one-way attack drones that fly autonomously to a target, widely used in Ukraine and by NATO forces. Demand has pulled forward the order book (moved future sales into the near term), and the company is expanding production capacity. As drone warfare becomes standard across modern militaries, demand for replenishment should stay strong.

### Satellites & Space Infrastructure

**[Rocket Lab (RKLB)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/RKLB)** — NASDAQ | Small-satellite launch & manufacturing | **Established**

Started as a small-satellite launch provider and is now a full satellite manufacturer. In 2026: won an $816 million Space Force contract to build 18 satellites, and a $190 million contract for 20 hypersonic test flights under the Pentagon's MACH-TB program. Q1 2026 revenue exceeded $200 million with $2.2 billion in backlog. The acquisition of Mynaric AG adds laser-based space communications hardware.

### Small Modular Reactors (SMR)

**[Oklo (OKLO)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/OKLO)** — NYSE | Advanced fission SMR developer | **Moonshot**

Developing the Aurora — a compact fast-fission (a type of nuclear reaction that uses fast-moving neutrons) reactor that can run on used nuclear fuel. Meta has signed a commitment for 1.2 gigawatts of Oklo power — one of the largest corporate clean-power deals ever announced. No commercial reactor is operating yet and NRC licensing (getting approval from the US nuclear safety regulator) is ongoing. Long-duration bet; high risk, high potential.

## Section 3: Sector Catalysts

*Big-picture events, policy changes, or spending announcements that can lift an entire sector — not just one company.*

**🔹 CHIPS Act 25% Tax Credit — Clock Runs Out December 31, 2026**

The US CHIPS and Science Act gives companies a 25% investment tax credit (get back 25 cents for every dollar spent) on new semiconductor manufacturing capacity — but only if the investment is *started* by December 31, 2026. This hard deadline is creating a burst of fab (semiconductor factory) construction commitments right now, benefiting chip equipment makers and materials suppliers like [Entegris (ENTG)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ENTG).

**🔹 European Chips Act 2.0 — Presented June 2026**

The European Commission unveiled a successor to its original chips law in June 2026, aiming to double Europe's share of global semiconductor production. This creates a second wave of government-subsidized (partly government-funded) chip-factory construction, extending the tailwind for [Entegris (ENTG)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ENTG) and [Coherent (COHR)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/COHR), which supply both US and European fabs.

**🔹 Project Vault — $12 Billion US Critical Minerals Reserve**

President Trump's Project Vault will build a domestic strategic reserve (a government-held stockpile, like the oil reserve) of all 60 minerals on the US critical minerals list, including all 17 rare earth elements. Backed by a $10 billion loan from the EXIM Bank (the Export-Import Bank — a government agency that provides trade financing) and $2 billion in private capital. Direct near-term revenue catalyst for [MP Materials (MP)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MP) and [USA Rare Earth (USAR)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/USAR). 54 countries attended the 2026 Critical Minerals Ministerial in Washington.

**🔹 AI Capex Supercycle — $527 Billion in FY2026**

The "Magnificent Seven" tech giants are expected to spend $527 billion on AI infrastructure and data centers in fiscal 2026 — up $62 billion from earlier estimates. Capex means capital expenditures — money spent on physical assets like buildings, servers, and equipment. Every dollar flows upstream to cooling companies, power equipment makers, and semiconductor materials suppliers. Next-generation AI server racks expected to draw 370 kilowatts of power, making advanced liquid cooling mandatory.

**🔹 DOE SMR Funding + 400 GW Nuclear Target**

The US Department of Energy has made $900 million available to accelerate commercial SMR deployment, with executive orders targeting 400 gigawatts of nuclear capacity by 2050 — roughly four times current US nuclear output. This creates a policy floor (ongoing government support) under the nuclear supply chain. Key beneficiaries: [BWX Technologies (BWXT)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/BWXT), [Oklo (OKLO)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/OKLO), and [X-Energy (XE)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XE).

**🔹 NATO Defense Spending at Record Highs + US Reshoring Wave**

Defense budgets across NATO are at record levels driven by the Russia-Ukraine conflict and global tensions. Rising tariffs and supply-chain concerns are pushing manufacturers to reshore (bring production back to the US). Both trends fuel demand for [AeroVironment (AVAV)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AVAV), [Rocket Lab (RKLB)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/RKLB), and industrial automation companies supplying newly built domestic factories.
