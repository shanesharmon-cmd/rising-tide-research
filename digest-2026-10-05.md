<h3>3. SECTOR CATALYSTS</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Power equipment shortage.</b> Transformers (the boxes that step electricity voltage up and down) can take years to get. Reports say about half of planned US data centers are delayed or canceled, and only about 5 GW of 12 GW announced this year is under construction. Good for makers of grid equipment, though it also slows the buildout. Equipment and cooling names like <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/VRT">Vertiv Holdings (VRT)</a> are in this lane.</li>
<li><b>Giant AI spending.</b> Reports say Alphabet, Amazon, Meta and Microsoft will spend $650 billion or more on AI capacity in 2026. Capex means money spent on buildings and equipment. Estimates vary by source ($320 billion to $725 billion), so treat the number as rough.</li>
<li><b>Missile defense (Golden Dome).</b> The Space Force has given out large satellite awards, including $1.75 billion to L3Harris and Sierra Space for 36 missile-tracking satellites. <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/LHX">L3Harris Technologies (LHX)</a> benefits directly, and smaller suppliers like <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/RKLB">Rocket Lab (RKLB)</a> can win subcontracts. <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/LMT">Lockheed Martin (LMT)</a> also won a $1.2 billion Army missile contract. Both are big firms, so they are mainly signals for their suppliers.</li>
