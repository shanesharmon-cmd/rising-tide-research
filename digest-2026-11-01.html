1. NEW CANDIDATES

ACM Research (ACMR) — Nasdaq, US/China operations | Chip-making equipment | Established
Makes machines that clean silicon wafers (the thin discs chips are cut from) during manufacturing. More chip factories means more cleaning machines. On Oct 28 it pre-announced (an early, unaudited estimate) third-quarter revenue of $360–365 million, about 34–36% higher than a year earlier. Full results are due on an earnings call Nov 5 (one source says Nov 6). Analysts had expected roughly $310–320 million, per secondary sources.

Teradyne (TER) — Nasdaq, US | Chip testing equipment | Established
Builds automated test machines that check chips work before they ship. UBS named it an under-the-radar AI play. Caution: it has already risen a lot, so check the price first.

USA Rare Earth (USAR) — Nasdaq, US | Rare earth magnets | Moonshot
Plans to make rare earth magnets (strong magnets used in motors, robots and cooling pumps) in the US. It signed a non-binding agreement (a statement of intent, not a firm order) with a pump maker for data-center cooling magnets. That news is from June 2025, and I found no newer update. It targeted starting production in 2026; I could not confirm whether it has.

2. UPCOMING IPOs

Terms: an IPO is when a private company first sells shares to the public. An S-1 filing is the paperwork filed with US regulators before going public. Confidentially filed means the paperwork was sent privately. A lockup date is when insiders become allowed to sell their shares, usually about 180 days after the IPO. That can push the price down.

  • Holtec Nuclear (HNUC) — nuclear plant equipment and services. Status: withdrawn. It postponed in mid-September, then withdrew the planned $825 million IPO (reported Sept 25). It never began trading, so there is no lockup date. It could return later.
  • X-Energy (XE) — small nuclear reactors. Status: priced (reported at $23 a share, about $1 billion raised). The April IPO implies a lockup expiry around late October 2026, but I could not confirm the exact date. Check if its shares have been affected.
  • Cerebras (AI chips) — status unclear: reports conflict between S-1 filed and re-filed confidentially. I could not confirm a ticker, so there is no Yahoo link yet.
  • Standard Nuclear (advanced nuclear fuel) — S-1 filed, no ticker found. HawkEye 360 (radio-signal satellite data) — reported as filed for an NYSE listing, no ticker found. SB Energy and CoVolt Power are named in a September IPO preview; status unconfirmed.
  • SpaceX — sources conflict on its status, so I won't report it.
