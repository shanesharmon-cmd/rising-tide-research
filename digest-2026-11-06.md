## 1. NEW CANDIDATES
- [ASML Holding (ASML)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ASML) — Netherlands, trades in the US as an ADR (a certificate that lets US investors buy a foreign stock). Chip-making equipment. Established. It makes the machines every advanced chip factory needs. Q3 sales were a record €11.7B; it guided Q4 to €14.2–15.2B and said customers are talking about orders for 2029–2030.
- [Powell Industries (POWL)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/POWL) — Nasdaq, US. Power/grid. Established. Makes switchgear (heavy electrical boxes that route and protect power). More data centers means more switchgear.
- [Comfort Systems USA (FIX)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/FIX) — NYSE, US. Cooling. Established. Installs heating and cooling systems, including for data centers.
- [nVent Electric (NVT)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/NVT) — NYSE, US. Cooling. Established. One source says its backlog (orders not yet delivered) rose to $2.6B, mostly data-center liquid cooling. Unverified.
- [NANO Nuclear Energy (NNE)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/NNE) — Nasdaq, US. Small modular reactors (SMRs: compact, factory-built nuclear plants). Moonshot. Won an AFWERX (US Air Force innovation office) small research award in July 2026 for its Kronos microreactor. No commercial reactor yet; ticker not independently confirmed.
