Rising Tide — Morning Scan

As of October 7, 2026. "Picks and shovels" means companies that sell the tools and parts big AI, space, and tech players need. Facts below come from quick web searches; I did not verify every figure, and none of this is financial advice.

1. NEW CANDIDATES

Vishay Precision Group (VPG) — NYSE, US | Robotics sensors | moonshot
Makes precision sensors (parts that let machines "feel" weight and force). One report says it had early design wins (suppliers chosen for future products) with humanoid-robot makers. Reported up about 216% this year, so it has already run hard. Risky to chase.

Jabil (JBL) — NYSE, US | Electronics manufacturing for AI infrastructure | established
Builds hardware for other companies. Reported $8.8 billion revenue last quarter and raised its full-year outlook because AI data-center demand is strong.

Ouster (OUST) — NYSE, US | Lidar (laser "eyes" for robots and vehicles) | moonshot
Sells sensors to robot and automation makers. Reported up about 130% this year. Profitability not confirmed.

Applied Materials (AMAT) — Nasdaq, US | Chip-making equipment | established
Sells the machines that make chips. It reported record revenue and said it expects its equipment business to grow over 30% in 2026 on AI demand.

Lam Research (LRCX) and KLA Corp (KLAC) — Nasdaq, US | Chip equipment | established
Also sell machines to chipmakers. When chipmakers build more factories, orders rise. Bank of America calls 2026 the midpoint of an "8–10 year" growth run for equipment makers.

ASML (ASML) — Nasdaq (ADR, a Dutch company's shares that trade in US dollars) | Chip lithography machines | established
Makes the machines that print the tiniest chip patterns. Citi and Bank of America both flagged it as well positioned for AI-driven growth. Likely already well known.

Sectors with nothing new today: rare earths, defense, space comms, biotech tooling (see prior digest of June 27 for earlier ideas).

2. UPCOMING IPOs

Terms: an IPO is when a private company first sells shares to the public. An S-1 filing is the paperwork filed with US regulators before that. A lockup date is when insiders become allowed to sell their shares, which can push the price down. Confidentially filed means the paperwork was sent privately first.

X-Energy (XE) — small nuclear reactor developer | priced April 24, 2026. Estimated lockup end around October 21, 2026 (my estimate from the usual 180 days, not confirmed). Watch for selling pressure.

SpaceX — space/satellites | priced in June 2026 at $135 per share, per search results. Its lockup is staggered: about 7% of shares unlock at days 70, 90, 105, 120 and 135 after the IPO, and Elon Musk's own shares are locked about 366 days (to about June 2027). Ticker not confirmed, so no link provided.

Cerebras Systems — AI chip maker | priced at $185 after an upsized $5.6 billion raise, then jumped 68% on day one, per search results. Ticker not confirmed, so no link provided. This replaces the "S-1 filed" status in my June digest.

Fervo Energy — geothermal power (heat from deep underground) for data centers | S-1 filed; Renaissance Capital estimates about $250 million raise. Latest status unconfirmed; no ticker yet.

This week's calendar (Oct 5): only a blank-check company (a shell that raises cash to buy a business later), Pine Tree Acquisition, and two small biotech/pharma names. Nothing clearly in our sectors. Renaissance Capital also notes the market is waiting on a possible Anthropic IPO.

3. SECTOR CATALYSTS

Chip equipment boom. Industry forecasts put 2026 equipment sales at a record $165.9 billion, up 23.2%. That lifts Applied Materials (AMAT), Lam Research (LRCX), KLA Corp (KLAC) and ASML (ASML).

Nuclear for data centers. Amazon, Google, and Microsoft keep signing deals for small nuclear reactors and restarted plants to power AI. This is a steady tailwind for reactor builders and parts suppliers such as X-Energy (XE). I found no brand-new rare earths policy news this run.

Year-end tax-credit deadline (from earlier digest, still applies). The US chip-factory tax credit requires construction to start by December 31, 2026, which may speed up equipment orders.
