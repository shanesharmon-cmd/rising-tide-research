1. NEW CANDIDATES

Chip-making equipment (the machines that build chips)

  • Applied Materials (AMAT) — NASDAQ, US — semiconductor equipment — established. It sells the machines chip factories use to make AI chips, so more AI chip orders mean more machine orders. Last reported quarter (August): adjusted earnings per share of $3.50 versus $3.39 expected, and it guided next-quarter sales to about $10.25 billion (per one news summary; verify).
  • Lam Research (LRCX) — NASDAQ, US — chip equipment — established. Makes machines that etch (carve) tiny patterns into chips. Reported up roughly 84% in 2026 before a pullback.
  • KLA Corp (KLAC) — NASDAQ, US — chip inspection tools — established. Its machines find defects in chips; more complex AI chips need more checking.
  • ASML Holding (ASML) — NASDAQ (ADR, a US-traded share of a foreign company), Netherlands — established. Sole maker of the most advanced chip-printing machines. One preview said its order book stretches into 2028 (unverified).

Heads-up: on Oct 13, chip-equipment stocks fell as investors took profits (Applied Materials about -7%, Lam and KLA about -4%). After big 2026 gains, prices are not cheap, so be careful chasing them.

Moonshots: nothing new today. Names from the last scan (MP Materials, Oklo, USA Rare Earth) were not re-verified.
