<h3>1. NEW CANDIDATES</h3>
<p><i>Established = already makes real money. Moonshot = newer and riskier.</i></p>
<ul>
<li><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/PH">Parker-Hannifin (PH)</a></b> — NYSE, USA — data-center cooling. <b>Established.</b> Makes the couplings and valves (small parts that connect and control the flow of liquid) used in liquid cooling. When data centers add liquid-cooled racks, they need more of these parts.</li>
<li><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TT">Trane Technologies (TT)</a></b> — NYSE, USA — data-center cooling. <b>Established.</b> Its building-cooling order backlog (orders confirmed but not yet delivered) grew by about $2.7 billion this year, helped by a roughly $1 billion purchase of a data-center cooling specialist, Stellar Energy, per a May 2026 report.</li>
<li><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ETN">Eaton (ETN)</a></b> — NYSE, USA — power and cooling. <b>Established.</b> Bought Boyd Thermal in March 2026 to add liquid cooling to its power equipment. Boyd is projected to add about $1.7 billion of revenue in 2026 (one report; unverified).</li>
<li><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TER">Teradyne (TER)</a></b> — NASDAQ, USA — chip testing and robotics. <b>Established.</b> Sells machines that test chips before they ship, and owns Universal Robots (factory robot arms). More AI chips means more testing.</li>
<li><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/CGNX">Cognex (CGNX)</a></b> — NASDAQ, USA — robotics and automation. <b>Established.</b> Makes machine-vision systems (cameras plus software that let robots "see" and inspect parts). Factories being built or reshored (moved back to the US) need them.</li>
<li><b><a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SYM">Symbotic (SYM)</a></b> — NASDAQ, USA — warehouse robots. <b>Moonshot.</b> Builds robotic warehouse systems, heavily tied to <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/WMT">Walmart (WMT)</a>. Key risk: depending on one big customer.</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. SECTOR CATALYSTS</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Chip equipment demand is running ahead of supply.</b> <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ASML">ASML (ASML)</a> reported record Q3 sales of €11.7 billion and raised its 2026 outlook to €44–45 billion. It warned 2027–2028 capacity may not meet demand. That helps equipment makers such as <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/LRCX">Lam Research (LRCX)</a>, <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AMAT">Applied Materials (AMAT)</a> and <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/KLAC">KLA (KLAC)</a>, and the parts makers who supply them. Analysts also tied it to higher spending by <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU">Micron (MU)</a>.</li>
<li><b>Rare earths:</b> The US government owns a stake in <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MP">MP Materials (MP)</a> and set a price floor of $110/kg on certain magnet metals through 2035 (per reports). Rare earths are metals used in magnets for motors and robots. This supports US suppliers.</li>
<li><b>Nuclear for data centers:</b> No new October deals found. Existing ones (<a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GOOGL">Alphabet/Google (GOOGL)</a> with private firm Kairos; <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AMZN">Amazon (AMZN)</a> with <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TLN">Talen Energy (TLN)</a>) keep the theme alive. <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/OKLO">Oklo (OKLO)</a> has non-binding letters of intent (not firm contracts) for 750 MW.</li>
<li><b>Robotics:</b> A report says the FCC moved to restrict imports of Chinese-made humanoid robots (date unconfirmed), which could help US makers.</li>
</ul>
