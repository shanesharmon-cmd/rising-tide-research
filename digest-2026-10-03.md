<h3>3. SECTOR CATALYSTS</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>First US small-reactor construction permit.</b> On Sept 29 the US nuclear regulator (the NRC) approved TVA's permit to build a 300-megawatt small modular reactor (SMR, a compact factory-built nuclear plant) in Tennessee. It used GE Vernova Hitachi's design, from <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GEV">GE Vernova (GEV)</a>. The review finished four months early. This is a good sign for the whole SMR supply chain, including <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/BWXT">BWX Technologies (BWXT)</a>, <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/OKLO">Oklo (OKLO)</a> and <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XE">X-Energy (XE)</a>.</li>
<li><b>Shortages through 2028.</b> Reports say HBM4 (very fast memory chips for AI) is sold out through next year, and power gear and liquid cooling are also scarce. Experts expect this to last until about 2028. Shortage means pricing power for suppliers such as <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/VRT">Vertiv Holdings (VRT)</a>.</li>
<li><b>China rare-earth pause ends soon.</b> China's wider export limits on some rare earths (metals used in magnets and chips) are suspended only until Nov 10, 2026. If they return, US producers like <a href="https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MP">MP Materials (MP)</a> could gain. The US Defense Department already agreed to buy all of MP's magnet output over 10 years with a price floor of $110/kg on one oxide.</li>
</ul>
