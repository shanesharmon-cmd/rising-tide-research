1. NEW CANDIDATES

  • Onto Innovation (ONTO) — Nasdaq, US — chip equipment. It makes tools that inspect chips for flaws. More AI chips and advanced chip packaging means more inspections. Established. An analyst cited by Yahoo Finance likes it for its exposure to HBM (high-bandwidth memory, the fast memory AI chips need).
  • Teradyne (TER) — Nasdaq, US — chip testing. It sells machines that test chips before they ship. One report says over 60% of its Q2 2026 revenue came from AI. Established. Risk: a June 2026 report said a parts shortage hit the stock.
  • Vertiv (VRT) — NYSE, US — data-center cooling and power gear. AI data centers run hot and need it. Established. It guided Q3 2026 sales (its own forecast) of $3.65–3.85 billion.
  • Vistra (VST) — NYSE, US — power plants. Data centers need huge amounts of electricity. Established. My search found only older coverage, so check current news.
  • MP Materials (MP) — NYSE, US — rare earths (metals used in strong magnets for motors and robots). The US government set a price floor of $110 per kilogram on certain products through 2035. Q1 2026 revenue was reported up 49% to $90.6 million. Moonshot-leaning: it is growing, but it is still scaling up.
  • USA Rare Earth (USAR) — Nasdaq, US — rare earth magnets. It signed a non-binding deal (an early, not-yet-firm agreement) with Moog to supply magnets for data-center cooling pumps. Moonshot. Its Oklahoma plant was expected to start production in early 2026; I could not confirm it did.
  • Oklo (OKLO) — NYSE, US — small modular reactors (SMRs, small nuclear plants built in factories). Tech firms want steady clean power. Moonshot. Q2 2026 loss was 28 cents a share, and analysts expect only about $3.8 million of revenue in 2026. A July report said shares were down about 47% for the year.
  • NANO Nuclear Energy (NNE) — Nasdaq, US — small reactors. Moonshot. I found no 2026 news, so treat as unverified.

2. UPCOMING IPOs

Terms: "S-1 filing" is the paperwork a company files before going public. "Confidentially filed" means the paperwork was filed privately. "Priced" means the share price is set and trading starts. A "lockup" is a period after the IPO when insiders cannot sell shares. When it ends, extra shares can hit the market.

3. SECTOR CATALYSTS

  • Chip equipment: Applied Materials (AMAT) reportedly raised its 2026 outlook for its chip-equipment segment to growth above 30%, up from 20%, on AI spending. Its CEO describes planning on a multi-year view. That helps toolmakers like Lam Research (LRCX) and KLA (KLAC). Risk: chip equipment demand rises and falls with customer spending.
  • Rare earths: In early September 2026, Chinese suppliers were reported to be refusing shipments to the US. That raises the value of US producers such as MP and USAR. The report is unconfirmed.
  • Nuclear for data centers: Google's deal with Kairos Power (a private company, so no ticker) backs 500 megawatts of small reactors, with power expected between 2030 and 2035. The deal date is not confirmed. It shows tech firms will pay for long-term power.
